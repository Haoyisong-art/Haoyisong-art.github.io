Daily Life (2022-2025)

This recent series explores the poetry of everyday objects and intimate spaces, investigating the complexities of identity, belonging, and emotional experience in contemporary life.

False Wonders

This painting depicts three forty-five-degree interlocking beds. The bed, as a very important and intimate piece of furniture in daily life, represents ordinary, everyday existence. The dominant colors are pure red, yellow, and blue, connected by a low-saturated blue-violet.

The work aims to deliver the beautiful dreams of peaceful life alongside the real pain and mismatched feelings of reality. Hope is expressed through harmonious colors and gentle textures, while uncomfortable feelings are sensed through thorns growing from the beds. The painting addresses the oxymoron in daily life as a female, lesbian immigrant, highlighting the need for sociocultural support for marginalized populations to fulfill their colorful dreams.

Oil on wood board, 48 x 48 inches
